# Batch 12 test results

## The short version

A sample of New Fast Tea Batch 12 was tested by QSS Inspection and Testing
Private Limited, an NABL-accredited laboratory in Thane. Seven synthetic
food colours were tested for by HPLC. All seven came back **Not Detected**.

That covers seven named colours, in one batch, by one method. It does not
mean the product has been tested for everything, and we are not going to
imply that it has. The limits of this report are set out further down.

## Why this test

In August 2026 an advisory was issued in respect of New Fast Tea **Batch
No. 10**, alleging added synthetic colour. Assurances from a brand about
its own product are worth very little, so we had product analysed by an
accredited third party instead.

Note the batch numbers: the advisory was about Batch 10, and this report
is on Batch 12. That distinction matters and we deal with it directly in
[the Batch 10 section of our lab testing hub](/transparency#batch-10).

## What "Not Detected" means

Not Detected is not a synonym for zero. It means the substance was not
found above the detection threshold of the HPLC method the laboratory used.
That is the honest ceiling on what any analytical test can tell you, and it
is a better thing to explain than to round up into a marketing claim.

The specification column on the report reads "should be absent" for all
seven parameters, which is the applicable requirement for synthetic
colours in tea. The result meets it.

## What this report does not cover

<Disclosure>
**Tartrazine was not tested.** Tartrazine is a common yellow synthetic
colour and it is not on this panel. Sunset Yellow FCF was tested and not
detected; Tartrazine was simply not part of the request. We are not
treating that as a pass.
[Why it is still untested →](/blog/tartrazine-gap)

**Only synthetic colours.** No pesticide residue, heavy metal, or
microbiological testing was carried out. Those are different tests.

**Only Batch 12.** Nothing here speaks to Batch 10, Batch 11, or any batch
after 12.

**Only this sample.** The laboratory's own note is explicit that the
results refer to the tested sample and applicable parameters. The report
also carries a broader concluding line about the product being safe for
consumption; we would rather you read that in the context of the lab's own
scope note than have us amplify it.
</Disclosure>
