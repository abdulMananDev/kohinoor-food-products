An advisory was raised about synthetic colouring in our instant mix, naming
Sunset Yellow FCF among others. It concerned **Batch No. 10**. The batch we
sent for analysis is **Batch No. 12**, packed on 25 July 2026, after the
advisory. Those are two different batches, and nothing below should be read
as a statement about Batch No. 10.

## What the laboratory tested

Seven synthetic dyes, each by HPLC, against a specification of “should be
absent”.

<Disclosure>

Tartrazine is not among the seven parameters in this report. If the advisory
named that compound specifically, it has not been tested yet the row above
says “not tested” rather than showing a pass, because those are not the same
thing.

Batch No. 10, the batch the advisory actually named, has not been retested.
We cannot make a claim about it and we are not going to imply one.

</Disclosure>
