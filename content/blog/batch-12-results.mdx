An advisory was raised about synthetic colouring in our instant mix, naming
[Sunset Yellow FCF](/blog/what-is-sunset-yellow-fcf) among others. It concerned **Batch No. 10**. The batch we
sent for analysis is **Batch No. 12**, packed on 25 July 2026, after the
advisory. Those are two different batches, and nothing below should be read
as a statement about Batch No. 10.

## What the laboratory tested

[Seven synthetic dyes](/blog/synthetic-colours-not-permitted-in-tea), each
by HPLC, against a specification of “should be absent”.

The laboratory's own conclusion, as printed: **“Based on the above results,
the New Fast Tea is safe for consumption.”**

QSS is accredited by NABL under certificate no. `TC-17494` to ISO/IEC
17025:2017, valid until 26 February 2027.

If you have not read a lab report before, [how to read an NABL lab
report](/blog/how-to-read-an-nabl-lab-report) walks through what each field on
that PDF means and how to verify the accreditation yourself.

<Disclosure>

Tartrazine is not among the seven parameters in this report. If the advisory
named that compound specifically, it has not been tested yet the row above
says “not tested” rather than showing a pass, because those are not the same
thing.

Batch No. 10, the batch the advisory actually named, has not been retested.
We cannot make a claim about it and we are not going to imply one.

</Disclosure>
