# Lab testing at New Fast Tea

In August 2026 an advisory was issued concerning New Fast Tea Batch No. 10.
Rather than respond with assurances, we had product tested at an
NABL-accredited laboratory and published the report.

This page holds every test we have commissioned, with dates, report
numbers, and the full PDFs. It is appended to, not rewritten.

## Batch 12 tested clear for seven synthetic colours

Batch 12 was packed on **25 July 2026**. A sample was received by QSS
Inspection and Testing Private Limited on **6 August 2026**, analysed
between 6 and 11 August, and reported on **11 August 2026** under report
number **OT/TEA/06-01/08/26**.

All seven synthetic colours on the panel were **Not Detected** by HPLC,
against a specification of "should be absent":

## What has not been tested

<Disclosure>
  - **Tartrazine.** Not on the Batch 12 panel. Tartrazine is one of the most
  widely used yellow food colours in India, and its absence from the panel is a
  real gap, not something we are glossing over. [More on this
  →](/blog/tartrazine-gap) - **Everything outside synthetic colours.** The Batch
  12 report covers seven colour parameters. It does not cover pesticide residue,
  heavy metals, or microbiological quality. Those are separate tests we have not
  commissioned. - **Batches other than 12.** Each batch stands on its own
  report.
</Disclosure>

## What happens next

Our packer has committed that every future batch will be tested at an
NABL-accredited laboratory before release, with sales beginning only after
a satisfactory report. Each of those reports will be added to this page
with its date, including any that come back unfavourable.

## Verifying any of this

QSS Inspection and Testing Private Limited holds NABL accreditation
TC-17494, issued 16 February 2026 and valid until 26 February 2027 so it
was accredited on the date the report was issued. Accreditation status can
be checked independently at nabl-india.org.
